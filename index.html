Research Focus
Production

Capsid Assembly

We use light and electron microscopy under genetic and pharmacological perturbations to understand the vector-host interactions critical for capsid assembly — the keys to producing enough rAAV to meet patient need.

Persistence

Gene Silencing

We use microscopy and proteomic approaches to understand how the host cell processes and regulates virally delivered genes — the keys to ensuring therapeutic transgenes are not silenced or eliminated over time.

Payload

DNA Concatenation

As part of the FNIH Bespoke Gene Therapy Consortium, we use genetic and pharmacological screens to identify host factors of concatenation — enabling much larger genes to be reconstituted in target tissues.
